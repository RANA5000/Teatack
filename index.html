Our Story

A Café Born of
Warmth & Wonder

Nestled along MG Road in the heart of Guwahati, Daphne's Café was built on a simple belief — that great food and genuine hospitality can transform an ordinary afternoon into something you carry with you forever.

From our Signature Chocolate Cake to hand-crafted mocktails and hearty mains, every item on our menu is a labour of love. We source fresh, think creative, and serve with pride.
